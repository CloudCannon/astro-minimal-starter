# 🚀 Welcome to the Astro Minimal Starter on CloudCannon

This is the **Astro Minimal Starter**, a pared-back Astro template wired up with CloudCannon's _Editable Regions_. Use it to explore how CloudCannon works — edit pages visually, write blog posts, and manage site data.

The **Astro Minimal Starter** is your sandbox, so go ahead and change anything (you can always make a new copy).

## 🖼️ Try the _Visual Editor_

[Edit the website home page](cloudcannon:collections/pages:/edit?collection=pages&path=%2Fsrc%2Fcontent%2Fpages%2Findex.md&schema=default&editor=visual&url=%2F) in CloudCannon's _Visual Editor_. Edit any text, image, or component directly on a preview of your webpage. Change a headline, swap a call-to-action, or rearrange page sections, all in context. You'll always know exactly what your website visitors will see.

## 🧩 Edit a page without front matter

The [about page](cloudcannon:collections/pages:/edit?collection=pages&path=%2Fsrc%2Fcontent%2Fpages%2Fabout.astro&schema=source_page&editor=visual&url=%2Fabout%2F) is a plain Astro template with no Markdown file behind it — its content lives right in the markup. CloudCannon's _source editables_ let you click and edit that content on the page, then write your changes straight back to the `.astro` file. It's the pattern to reach for when a developer wants full control of a page's markup while still handing editors a visual editing experience.

## ✏️ Write in the _Content Editor_

Use CloudCannon's _Content Editor_ when you need a clean, Markdown-based writing experience. Take a look at the [starter's blog collection](cloudcannon:collections/blog) and click any post to start editing! With a familiar formatting toolbar and no distractions, you'll be updating your website content in no time.

## 🖥️ Customize website data with the _Data Editor_

Open the files in the [starter's data collection](cloudcannon:collections/data) in CloudCannon's _Data Editor_ to update the links in your header navigation, the content in your footer, the tags available to your blog posts, or your website's SEO information. Changes you make in the _Data Editor_ flow through to every page automatically.

## 🔒 Your changes are safe

Don't worry — CloudCannon preserves every edit you make and, when you are ready, will update your _Git Repository_. Use the _Save_ button in the top right of the app to see those changes live on your website.

## ☁️ What's next?

Once you've explored what CloudCannon can do, you're ready for the real thing — getting your own website on CloudCannon. Head back to your [Organization home](cloudcannon:!home). The **Getting Started** guide will walk you through connecting your own _Git Repository_ and setting up WYSIWYG editing.
